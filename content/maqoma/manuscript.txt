Le ncwadi ibalisa ngobomi bukaMaqoma, inkokeli yamaXhosa eyaphila ngexesha lokwanda kolawulo lobukoloniyali eKapa. Ibhalelwe ulutsha nabantu abadala. Sisebenzisa iinkcukacha ezikwimithombo echaziweyo, size sahlule imbali kwimifanekiso eyakhiwe ngobugcisa. Akukho ntetho siyifake emlonyeni wakhe. I · Ukukhula nobunkokeli
1 · Ukukhulela kwixesha lenguqu

UMaqoma wazalwa malunga nowe-1798. Wayengunyana kaNgqika, inkokeli yamaRharhabe. Namhlanje igama lakhe lisikhokelela kwimbali yomhlaba, yobunkokeli neyokuchasa ukwandiswa kolawulo lobukoloniyali. Kodwa ukuqonda umntu kufuna okungakumbi kunokwazi igama lakhe. Kufuneka sijonge ixesha awayephila kulo, abantu awayenxulumene nabo, neziganeko ezaziguqula ilizwe lakhe. Le ncwadi iqala apho: phakathi kwabantu nomhlaba, ingaqali ngomfanekiso weqhawe elingenazinye iinkalo. Imihla esiyibhalayo isekelwe kwimithombo edweliswe ekupheleni; apho kukho ukungavumelani, asikufihli oko.

Imilambo neentaba zale mbali azingomhombiso nje. Zisinceda sibone indawo ekwakuhlalwa kuyo, neendawo ezaziphikiswa xa imida yobukoloniyali yayisanda. Xa sijonga umfanekiso womhlaba, masikhumbule ukuba awusixeleli yonke into ngobomi babantu ababekuloo ndawo. Izindlu esizibonisayo, ukukhanya, neendlela ezihanjwa ngabantu zakhiwe ngobugcisa. Asizibizi ngokuba ngumfanekiso ochanekileyo wosuku oluthile. Sisebenzisa imifanekiso ukuvula indlela yokufunda, size sisebenzise imithombo ukucacisa oko sinokukuthetha ngembali. Umahluko phakathi kwezi zinto ubalulekile kumfundi nakumdali.

I · Ukukhula nobunkokeli
2 · Ukuthwala uxanduva

Emva kokusweleka kukaNgqika ngowe-1829, uMaqoma waba libambela ngexesha uSandile wayesemncinane. I-Amathole Museum ibeka eli xesha phakathi kowe-1829 nowe-1840. USandile wayengumninawa wakhe ngoyise, yaye kamva wathatha ubunkokeli. Ezi nkcukacha zisinceda singacingi ukuba zonke izikhundla zazifana, okanye ukuba umntu omnye wayemele wonke umntu ngalo lonke ixesha. Ubunkokeli kufuneka bujongwe ngokwexesha nendawo. Apha asongezi izithethe, iinkqubo okanye amazwi esingenabungqina bawo; sigcina ingqalelo kulwalamano oluchazwe kwimithombo esiyifundileyo.

Ukubaliswa kwembali kudla ngokubeka inkokeli embindini womfanekiso. Kule ncwadi sifuna umfundi abone nabantu abamngqongileyo. Imifanekiso yabo ayibonisi abantu esinokubachaza ngamagama avela kumaxwebhu. Ngabantu abakhiwe ngobugcisa ukuze indawo ingabonakali ingenabomi. Asibaniki iingcinga okanye iintetho esithi zenzeka ngokwenene. Lo ngumda esiwubekayo: umntu womfanekiso unokunceda ukubalisa, kodwa akanakuthathwa njengengqina lembali. Ukuthwala uxanduva lokubalisa kuthetha nokwazi ixesha lokuyeka, nokuvuma ukuba kukho izinto esingazaziyo.

II · Umhlaba nokugxothwa
3 · Umhlaba ngasemlanjeni

Ngowe-1822 uMaqoma wakha indawo yakhe yolawulo ngaseKat River. I-South African History Online ichaza oku njengenxalenye yokubuyela kumhlaba owawuphikiswa. Umhlaba ophakathi kweFish River neKeiskamma wawusembindini wengxabano nolawulo lwaseKapa. Xa amagama emilambo ebhalwa ephepheni, anokubonakala ngathi yimigca nje. Kodwa ibali lokuhlala emhlabeni alinakuphela kumgca okwimephu. Kule ncwadi amagama eendawo agcinwa ngendlela anokulandelelwa ngayo kwimithombo, ukuze umfundi akwazi ukubuyela kuyo aze aqhubeke nokuphanda ngokwakhe. Umbuzo womhlaba awumi wodwa kule mbali. Udibana nombuzo wokuba ngubani owayenegunya lokumisela imida, nokuba abantu ababehlala apho babejongana njani nezigqibo ezazithathwa ngamagunya awayesanda. La maphepha abeka loo mibuzo phambi komfundi.

Khawujonge indlela umlambo owela ngayo emfanekisweni. Lo ngumfanekiso ocingelweyo wendawo, hayi imephu enokulinganiswa. Awubonisi imida eqinisekisiweyo, okanye ilungelo lomntu eliqinisekiswe yile ncwadi. Usimema ukuba sicinge ngobudlelwane phakathi kwendawo nobomi. Imbali yokugxothwa ayifanele ibaliswe ngathi abantu babesuka kwenye indawo baye kwenye ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa. Noko ke, ukuze sihloniphe ubungqina, asibali ilahleko yosapho oluthile esingenalo irekhodi lalo. Sishiya isithuba sokuba uphando olongezelelekileyo luzise iinkcukacha ezingekabikho kule drafti.

II · Umhlaba nokugxothwa
4 · Ukugxothwa nokubuya

Ngowe-1829 imikhosi yobukoloniyali yamgxotha uMaqoma kummandla awayehlala kuwo. I-Amathole Museum iphinda ichaze ukugxothwa kwakhe ngowe-1833. Oku akusosiganeko esinye esinomhla omnye kuphela: kubonisa uxinzelelo olwaluphindaphinda. Kule ngxelo umhlaba uhlala ubuyela embindini, kuba ukugxothwa kwakunxulumene nokwanda kolawulo lwaseKapa. Asifaki ntetho ethi yathethwa ngexesha lokuhamba. Endaweni yoko, sibeka iziganeko ngokulandelelana kwazo, ukuze umfundi abone ukuba le ngxabano yakhula njani ngaphambi kweemfazwe ezalandelayo.

Umfanekiso wabantu abasendleleni apha ngumfanekiso wokwakha ukuqonda, kungekhona umzobo othathwe kwirekhodi losapho oluthile. Izinto abaziphetheyo zikhethwe ngumzobi, azichazwanga njengoluhlu lwezinto ezathathwa ngaloo mini. Oku kubalulekile xa sibalisa ngokulahlekelwa: ubuhle bomfanekiso mabungasifihli isidingo sobungqina. Sinokuma sijonge indawo engenabantu ngaphambili, kodwa masingacingi ukuba ukuthula kwayo kuthetha ukuba akukho mbali. Ukubuza ukuba ngubani owayelapho, ngubani owanyanzelwa ukuba ahambe, yaye ngubani owabhala ingxelo, yindlela yokufunda ngononophelo.

III · Ukuchasa namacebo emfazwe
5 · Ukuchasa ukwandiswa kolawulo

Kwimfazwe yowe-1834 ukuya kowe-1835, uMaqoma wathatha inxaxheba ekuchaseni ukwandiswa kolawulo lobukoloniyali. Imithombo esiyisebenzisayo inxulumanisa le ngxabano nokuhluthwa komhlaba. Apha asizami ukuguqula imfazwe ibe ngumboniso woloyiso kuphela. Ukuyiqonda kufuna ukubona izinto ezaziphikiswa, amandla awayengalingani, nexesha elide lengxabano. Akukho mazwi omyalelo okanye incoko yamajoni equlunqiweyo kule ncwadi. Into esiyibhala njengembali kufuneka ikwazi ukulandela umkhondo wayo iye kumthombo, kungekhona ekubeni ibonakale inomtsalane kwiphepha.

Kwiminyaka eyalandelayo ingxabano ayizange iphele. Iindlela zokuchasa, zokuthetha nokufuna uxolo zaziyinxalenye yembali ende. Umfanekiso wenkokeli ejonge kwindawo ekude unokubonakala usixelela iingcinga zayo, kodwa awusixeleli zona. Ngumsebenzi wobugcisa, hayi ubungqina bento eyayisentliziyweni kaMaqoma. Ngenxa yoko asibhali ukuba wayecinga ntoni ngelo xesha. Sigcina umbhalo kwinto echazwe yimithombo, size sivumele umfundi aqonde umahluko phakathi kokutolika umfanekiso nokwazi isiganeko. Imbali iyomelela xa loo mahluko ungacinywanga.

III · Ukuchasa namacebo emfazwe
6 · Iintaba zaseWaterkloof

Ngexesha lemfazwe yowe-1850 ukuya kowe-1853, uMaqoma wakhokela ukuchasa kwiintaba nakumahlathi aseWaterkloof. Iingxelo zembali zigxininisa indlela awayesebenzisa ngayo le ndawo kwiphulo lakhe. Asibonisi isicwangciso esineenkcukacha zohlaselo oluthile, kuba asiphindanga salwakha ngamaxwebhu alo. Endaweni yoko, umfanekiso ugxininisa ukuphakama kweentaba, ukuxinana kwemithi nobunzima bokuhamba. Indawo iyinxalenye yembali, ingengomva ongabalulekanga. Umfundi unokuyibona le nto ngaphandle kokuba umfanekiso uzenze imephu yomkhosi okanye ubungqina beqhinga elithile. Esi sigaba sasiyinxalenye yemfazwe ekwabizwa ngokuba yiMfazwe kaMlanjeni. Igama lendawo nelomfazwe ligcinwa apha ukuze umfundi akwazi ukunxulumanisa esi sahluko nezinye iingxelo, angacingi ukuba libali elizimeleyo ngaphandle kweziganeko ezazisenzeka ngexesha elinye.

I-Amathole Museum ichaza ukuba uMaqoma wathethathethana ngoxolo ngoMatshi wowe-1853. Ukufika kweli nqanaba akucimi iminyaka yokulwa eyandulelayo. Kwaye ukuphela kwemfazwe akusixeleli, kukodwa, ukuba bonke abantu babuyela kubomi ababebuphila ngaphambili. Iphepha lethu liyama apha ukuze litshintshe umbuzo: emva kweengxelo zamadabi, sibabona njani abantu ababephila kuloo mimandla? Ukubalisa ngenkokeli akufanele kusenze silibale olu lwimi lobomi bemihla ngemihla. Amaphepha alandelayo ajonga lo mbuzo ngaphandle kokuyila ubungqina obutsha ngeentsapho ezithile.

IV · Uluntu nemiphumo
7 · Ngaphaya kwamadabi

Imfazwe inokubhalwa ngemihla, ngamagama eenkokeli nangamagama eendawo. Kodwa loo ndlela yokubhala ayanelanga yodwa. Ukugxothwa emhlabeni kusenza sibuze nangabantu abangabonakali ngokucacileyo kumaxwebhu esinawo. Ngubani owayegcina ikhaya? Ngubani owayezama ukuqhubeka nobomi emva kokuhamba? Le mibuzo ayinampendulo eyilwe kule ncwadi. Siyibeka ukuze umfundi abone ukuba umlando omfutshane unemiqathango. Ukungabikho kwegama kwirekhodi esilifundileyo akuthethi ukuba umntu lowo wayengabalulekanga, okanye ukuba ubomi bakhe abufanelanga kukhunjulwa.

Lo mfanekiso wamaqela abantu awubangi ukuba bonke babephila ngendlela enye okanye benezimvo ezifanayo. Iinkcukacha zobuso neentshukumo zakhiwe ukuze iphepha lifundeke, hayi ukuze zibe luhlobo olutsha lobungqina. Umfundi unokufunda imbali ngokubuza ukuba umfanekiso ubonisa ntoni na, kwaye yintoni ongakwaziyo ukuyiqinisekisa. Yiloo nto sibeka amanqaku emithombo ecaleni kwenkqubo yokudala. Umsebenzi wobugcisa unokuba mhle kwaye uchukumise, kodwa ubuhle bawo abuyithathi indawo yophando. Ukubaliswa ngentlonipho kufuna ukuba ezi zinto zihambe kunye.

IV · Uluntu nemiphumo
8 · Xa imithombo ingavumelani

Ngowe-1856 nowe-1857 kwenzeka intlekele yokuxhelwa kweenkomo phakathi kwamaXhosa. Indima kaMaqoma kule ngxaki ayichazwa ngendlela enye kwimithombo esiyifundileyo. Omnye umthombo uthi wathatha inxaxheba; omnye uchaza ubungqina obubonisa ukuba kusenokwenzeka ukuba wayechasa ngokufihlakeleyo. Asikhethi elinye ibali ngokungathi ingxoxo iphelile. Sibhala umahluko kumanqaku ophando. Asibonisi isiprofeto, isithethe okanye incoko esithi yenzeka. Olu khetho alucimi ubunzima bexesha; lugcina umda phakathi koko kubhaliweyo noko bekunokuyilwa.

Xa imithombo ingavumelani, umsebenzi womdali awupheleli ekukhetheni inguqulelo enomtsalane ngakumbi. Unokugcina iingxelo zahlukene, abonise ukuba ngubani ozibhalileyo, aze abhale umbuzo ongakaphendulwa. Kule ncwadi sikhetha loo ndlela. Irekhodi lophando linokwandiswa kamva xa kufumaneka ubungqina obutsha okanye uphononongo olwenziwa ngumntu onolwazi oluthe vetshe. Asithi le drafti lilizwi lokugqibela. Umfundi uyamenywa ukuba alandele imithombo, aqaphele imida yayo, aze abone ukuba ukufunda imbali kungumsebenzi oqhubekayo, onoxanduva lokulungisa xa kufuneka.

V · Ukuvalelwa nokubuya
9 · Ukusiwa eRobben Island

Ukusiwa kukaMaqoma eRobben Island kubekwa kowe-1857 yi-Amathole Museum, kanti umgca wexesha weRobben Island Museum ubhala ukufika kwakhe ngowe-1858. Asidibanisi le mihla sithi umba sele usonjululwe. Sibhala u-1857/1858 ukuze umfundi awubone umahluko. UKatyi, inkosikazi yakhe, naye wayekwesi siqithi ngexesha lokuvalelwa kokuqala. Umfanekiso wolwandle awubonisi uhambo olurekhodiweyo ngolo hlobo: uyindlela yobugcisa yokubonisa ukwahlulwa phakathi kwesiqithi nomhlaba omkhulu, ungengobungqina benqanawa, bemozulu okanye bendlela awayehleli ngayo ngexesha lohambo.

I-Robben Island Museum ichaza ukuba iinkosi zazihlaliswa kwizindlu ezigqunywe ngeeseyile. Asisebenzisi umfanekiso wesisele sanamhlanje ukuze simele eli xesha. Sekunjalo, izindlu ezikwimizobo yethu azisiyomilinganiselo eqinisekisiweyo yesakhiwo esithile. Ziseyingcaciso eyenziwe ngobugcisa. Ukucacisa oku kukhusela umfundi ekuxubeni amaxesha ahlukeneyo. Isiqithi sinembali ende; le ncwadi ijolise kwixesha likaMaqoma. Asongezi imisebenzi yakhe yosuku, ukutya kwakhe okanye amazwi awawathethayo, kuba ezo nkcukacha azisekelwanga kwiirekhodi eziphononongwe kulo msebenzi. Ulwandle oluphakathi kwesiqithi nomhlaba omkhulu luyabonakala emfanekisweni, kodwa iminyaka yokuvalelwa ayinakulinganiswa ngumfanekiso omnye. Ngoko eli phepha lifundwa kunye nomgca wexesha, ukuze ixesha elide lingaguqulwa libe ngumzuzu omfutshane nje wokujonga indawo.

V · Ukuvalelwa nokubuya
10 · Ukubuyela kumhlaba omkhulu

UMaqoma wakhululwa ngowe-1869, emva kweminyaka emininzi eRobben Island. I-Nelson Mandela Foundation ichaza iminyaka elishumi elinesibini yokuvalelwa kwakhe. Ukuphuma entolongweni akuzange kuthethe ukuba ingxabano yomhlaba iphelile. Ngokwengxelo yeRobben Island Museum, wazama ukubuyela kumhlaba wakhe kufutshane neBlinkwater, kummandla weKat River. Le ngxelo idibanisa ukubuya kwakhe nembali yokuhluthwa komhlaba eyandulelayo. Umfanekiso wokujonga elunxwemeni usetyenziswa njengotshintsho phakathi kwamaphepha; awubangi ukuba uyawazi umzuzu othile wokufika kwakhe okanye iingcinga awayenazo ngaloo mini.

Xa umntu ebuyela kwindawo emva kweminyaka emininzi, umbuzo wokuba yintoni eseleyo unamandla kumfundi. Kodwa apha asiwuphenduli ngokuyila inkumbulo kaMaqoma. Sibonisa umhlaba, size sibeke ecaleni kwawo iinkcukacha esinazo. Umfundi makakwazi ukwahlula phakathi kwengcamango evuswa libali nengxelo exhaswa ngumthombo. Le yindlela yokugcina umntu wembali engaguqulwa abe ngumlinganiswa esinokumbhalela nantoni na. Ibali linokufundeka kakuhle ngaphandle kokuba simnike incoko okanye isithembiso esingazange siqinisekiswe. Ukungayili nako sisigqibo sokudala.

VI · Ukuvalelwa kokugqibela nenkumbulo
11 · Ukuvalelwa okwesibini

Ngowe-1871 uMaqoma wabuyiselwa eRobben Island. Umgca wexesha wemyuziyam uchaza ukuba ngeli xesha wayengenaye uKatyi. Ukubuyiselwa kwakhe kwesi siqithi kuvala inxalenye enzima yebali lokubuya kwakhe emhlabeni omkhulu. Sibhala oku ngokuthe ngqo, singafaki ntetho yokuvalelisa okanye umfanekiso esithi ngumzuzu wokugqibela kunye. Isithuba esingazaliswanga ngamazwi sinokuhlala sikhona kwincwadi. Asidingi ukusigcwalisa ukuze ibali libe nentsingiselo. Imida yobungqina iyinxalenye yomsebenzi wethu, njengemigca nemibala esiyisebenzisayo ukwenza iphepha.

UMaqoma wasweleka eRobben Island ngomhla we-9 kuSeptemba wowe-1873. Le ncwadi ayichazi unobangela wokufa kwakhe. Ayibonisi ibhedi yakhe yokugqibela, umntu owayekunye naye okanye amazwi akhe okugqibela. Ezo nkcukacha azikaqinisekiswa kulo msebenzi. Endaweni yoko, umfanekiso ujonga ulwandle nendawo, ushiye imbali ingongezwanga ngomboniso owenziwe ngathi uyinyaniso. Ukukhumbula umntu akusinyanzeli ukuba siyazi yonke into ngaye. Kunokusinyanzela ukuba sithethe ngokucacileyo ngaloo nto siyaziyo, size singayifihli into esingayaziyo.

VI · Ukuvalelwa kokugqibela nenkumbulo
12 · Igama eliqhubeka lifundwa

Ubomi bukaMaqoma buqhubeka bufundwa kwiincwadi, kwiimyuziyam nakuphando lweembali ezidluliselwa ngomlomo. UTimothy J. Stapleton wabhala ngenkumbulo kaMaqoma kwizithethe zomlomo zamaJingqi. Kulo msebenzi sifunde inkcazelo yoluntu neereferensi ezibonakalayo zelo nqaku, asifundanga umbhalo walo opheleleyo. Asiboleki mazwi abantu ekwadliwanondlebe nabo. Ukukhankanya uphando akusiniki imvume yokusebenzisa nayiphi na ingxelo ngokungakhathali. Kusikhumbuza ukuba imbali iphila nakubantu, kwaye ukufunda ngakumbi kufuna ukumamela, ukucela imvume xa ifuneka nokugcina imvelaphi yengxelo icacile. Ukugcina amagama emithombo kubalulekile. Kuvumela umntu ofunda ngesiXhosa ukuba abone apho kuvela khona ingxelo, nokuba umthombo ubhalwe ngolunye ulwimi. Ulwimi lwencwadi malungabi sisithintelo ekufikeleleni kuphando; malube yenye indlela yokuluvula kubafundi.

Le ncwadi iphela ngomhlaba ovulekileyo, ingapheli ngombuzo wokuba ngubani ophumeleleyo kuphela. Ubomi bukaMaqoma busibuyisela kubunkokeli, ekuchaseni ukuhluthwa komhlaba nakwindlela esikhumbula ngayo abantu. Kumfundi, eli liphepha lokugqibela lebali; kumdali, kusekho umsebenzi wokuphonononga ulwimi, imithombo nemifanekiso. Kwi-Intsomi Studio la manqanaba agcinwa enxulumene, ukuze umbhalo ulungiswe ngaphandle kokuphulukana nomkhondo wawo. Ibali lethu liyidrafti yomboniso. Injongo yalo kukuvula indlela yokufunda nokudala ngesiXhosa, kuhlonitshwe abantu bembali nabafundi abaza kulifunda.
